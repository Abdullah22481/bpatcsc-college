About Us

Formed to serve, structured to educate.

The Bangladesh Public Administration Training Centre School & College (BPATCSC) operates under the Bangladesh Public Administration Training Centre (BPATC), Savar, Dhaka. Established to serve the children of BPATC trainees, staff, and the surrounding community, the institute runs a combined school and college programme within a single, unified campus.

The institute follows the national curriculum prescribed by the relevant education boards, while maintaining an internal culture of punctuality, discipline, and measurable academic outcomes. Both the school and college sections operate under common administrative oversight, ensuring consistency in policy, conduct, and academic standards across all classes.

Admission is open to students in accordance with the institute's published eligibility criteria and seat allocation policy, with priority consideration extended as per BPATC's internal guidelines.

Mission & Vision

A clear direction for the institute.

Vision

To be recognised as a disciplined, high-performing school and college that produces well-rounded students prepared for both higher education and responsible citizenship.

Mission

To deliver a structured academic environment that combines rigorous classroom instruction with the institutional values of discipline, accountability, and service — supporting every student's academic, moral, and personal development from entry through to their final examinations.

Principal's Message

A word from the Principal.

It is my privilege to lead an institute built on discipline, service, and academic integrity. Our students are the children of civil servants and members of our surrounding community, and we consider it our duty to prepare each one of them — academically and morally — for the responsibilities they will carry forward.

We remain committed to maintaining transparent administration, a safe campus environment, and a teaching staff dedicated to the individual progress of every student. I welcome all guardians and students to engage closely with the institute as active partners in this process.

— Principal, BPATCSC

Governing Body

Institutional oversight and management.

The institute is administered under the general supervision of BPATC, with academic and administrative affairs guided by a Governing Body comprising senior BPATC officials, the Principal, and nominated faculty and guardian representatives. The Governing Body is responsible for policy oversight, budget approval, staff appointments, and ensuring the institute's continued compliance with national education board regulations.
