Academic Policy

Rules & Regulations

All students are expected to maintain punctuality, discipline, and respectful conduct on campus at all times. Attendance is recorded daily, and a minimum attendance threshold is required to sit for internal and board examinations. Repeated absence without valid justification is addressed through the guardian and, where necessary, the Governing Body.

Use of mobile phones during class hours, unauthorised absence from assigned periods, and any form of misconduct on campus are subject to disciplinary action as outlined in the institute's code of conduct, available from the administrative office.

Academic Policy

Teaching Process

Instruction follows a structured lesson-planning framework aligned with the national curriculum for both the school and college sections. Each subject teacher maintains a term-wise syllabus breakdown, supported by regular class tests, assignments, and continuous assessment to track individual student progress.

Remedial classes are arranged for students who require additional support, and subject teachers hold periodic consultation hours for guardians to review academic performance directly.

Academic Policy

Examination Process

Class Tests & Assignments

Conducted throughout each term to support continuous, formative assessment.

Half-Yearly Examination

A comprehensive internal examination held mid-session across all subjects.

Pre-Test / Test Examination

Conducted ahead of board examinations to gauge readiness and identify weak areas.

Final / Board Examination

Scheduled and administered in accordance with the respective national education board.

Academic Policy

Subject Change

Requests to change a subject or group must be submitted in writing to the Academic Coordinator within the deadline announced at the start of each academic session. Requests are considered based on seat availability, academic performance in the current subject, and written consent from the student's guardian.

Subject changes are not permitted after the mid-term assessment has been conducted for the relevant subject, except under exceptional circumstances approved by the Principal.

Campus Life

Features

Unified Campus

School and college sections share a single, well-maintained campus with dedicated facilities.

Disciplined Environment

A structured daily routine built around punctuality, attendance, and conduct standards.

Co-curricular Activities

Regular assemblies, debate, sports, and cultural programmes throughout the academic year.

Guardian Engagement

Scheduled guardian meetings and progress reviews each term.

Campus Life

College Dress

All students are required to wear the prescribed uniform on campus during institute hours. Uniform specifications, including seasonal variations, are issued by the administrative office at the start of each academic year and are strictly enforced as part of the institute's discipline policy.

Students appearing without the prescribed uniform, without prior written permission, may be marked absent for the day at the discretion of the class teacher.

Campus Life

Identity Card

Every enrolled student is issued an official identity card at the start of the academic session. The card must be carried on campus at all times and presented on request by any member of staff or campus security.

Loss of an identity card must be reported immediately to the administrative office. A duplicate card may be issued upon written application and payment of the applicable reissue fee.

Campus Life

Transportation

Institute transport is available on a limited number of designated routes for students residing within the BPATC campus and surrounding areas. Seats are allocated on a first-come basis, subject to route capacity, and require an annual subscription arranged through the administrative office.

Students availing institute transport are expected to follow the conduct guidelines set out for transport users, including punctual arrival at designated pickup points.

Resources

Library

The institute library maintains a quiet, well-catalogued collection of textbooks, reference materials, and supplementary reading supporting both the school and college curricula. Students may borrow materials in accordance with the library's lending policy, and reading space is available during designated library periods and free class hours.

Resources

Laboratory

Dedicated science laboratories support practical coursework in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology for both school and college students. Practical sessions are conducted under staff supervision in accordance with the safety protocols and syllabus requirements set by the respective education boards.
