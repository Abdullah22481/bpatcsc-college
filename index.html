About the institute

Formed to serve, structured to educate.

BPATCSC operates under the Bangladesh Public Administration Training Centre, offering a combined school and college programme for the children of trainees and the surrounding community. The institute maintains an academic culture built on punctuality, discipline, and measurable outcomes.
